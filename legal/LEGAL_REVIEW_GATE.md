# Legal review gate

## Required before production

The institution/legal reviewer must approve:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Refund/Cancellation Policy
- Admission Terms
- Parent/Guardian Consent Notice
- Student/Minor Data Handling Process
- Payment Terms
- Cookie/Tracking Notice
- Marketing consent wording
- Testimonials/photo/video permissions
- Claims and advertising copy

## Questions for legal review

1. What is the exact legal entity name?
2. What is the entity's registered address?
3. What entity type applies?
4. Who is the contracting party for course purchases?
5. Who is responsible for the website/data processing?
6. What is the privacy/grievance contact?
7. What is the refund/cancellation policy?
8. What consumer/e-commerce requirements apply to this business model?
9. Does any user-generated-content feature make IT Rules intermediary obligations relevant?
10. What parent/guardian consent method should be used for minors?
11. What vendors/processors receive personal data?
12. What retention periods should apply?
13. Are international vendors/transfers involved?
14. Are testimonials/photos/videos licensed and consented?
15. What claims can the academy substantiate?

## Release blocker

If legal review is incomplete for a feature that materially affects rights, payments, privacy or minors, do not release that feature to production.
