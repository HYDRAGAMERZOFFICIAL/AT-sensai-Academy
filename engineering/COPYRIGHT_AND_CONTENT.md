# Copyright & Content Safety

## Allowed sources

- Institution-owned content.
- Content supplied with explicit permission/license.
- Properly licensed stock/media.
- Original content created for AT Sensei.
- Public-domain material where status is verified.

## Prohibited

- Copying competitor course pages.
- Scraping another institute's syllabus text.
- Copying books or paid study material.
- Reusing images without license.
- Reusing student photographs without permission.
- Uploading pirated PDFs.
- Using copyrighted exam material without checking permission.

## AI-generated content

AI can draft, restructure or assist, but:
- factual claims must be verified;
- copyrighted text should not be reproduced;
- institutional voice/content needs approval;
- legal text requires legal review.

Maintain the asset register.
