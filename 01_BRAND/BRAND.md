# AT Sensei Academy — Brand Specification

## Official name
AT Sensei Academy

## Tagline
LEARN TODAY, LEAD TOMORROW

## Brand positioning from supplied catalog
Right awareness + right guidance + systematic preparation = a better career.

The academy presents itself around career awareness, structured learning, expert mentorship and exam-oriented preparation.

## Visual direction
Modern Academic + Premium + Trustworthy + Aspirational.

Do not reproduce the catalog page layouts literally. Preserve its identity while modernizing spacing, typography, navigation, cards and responsive behavior.

## Digital color approximation
These are approximations from the supplied catalog, NOT official brand HEX values:
- Deep Navy: #071A4D
- Academy Blue: #5367B8
- Sensei Red: #D92820
- Gold/Yellow: #F2D12E
- White: #FFFFFF
- Soft background: #F5F7FB
- Primary text: #172033

Before launch, obtain official brand colors if the institution has brand guidelines.

## Typography recommendation
- UI/body: Inter or Manrope
- Display: Manrope ExtraBold
- Motivational accent: Playfair Display Italic

## Color usage
Navy = primary structure.
Blue = secondary surfaces and accents.
Red = restrained brand accent, especially around “Sensei”.
Gold = small success/highlight accents.
White/light gray = breathing room.

Avoid excessive gradients, excessive red, cluttered banners and stock-photo-heavy layouts.
