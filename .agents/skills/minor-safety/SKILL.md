# Skill: Minor / Student Data

Use for Foundation/student features.

Rules:
- Assume some Foundation users may be minors.
- Minimize child data.
- Avoid public profiles.
- Use parent/guardian workflow where required.
- Keep consent evidence.
- Avoid targeted advertising/profiling.
- Restrict staff access.
- Do not collect government ID for convenience.
- Require legal/product approval for age or parent verification design.
