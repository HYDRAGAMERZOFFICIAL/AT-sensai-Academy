# Career & Competitive Exam Awareness Workshop

## Catalog description
An awareness initiative designed to help students discover career opportunities, understand the competitive-exam landscape and take an informed first step towards their future.

## Catalog details
Duration: 45–90 Minutes
Audience: Students 8–10, PUC, Degree + active aspirants
Cost: 100% FREE

## Outcomes listed
- Awareness of Government career opportunities
- Understanding of competitive-exam pathways
- Clarity on preparation and career direction
- Motivation to begin systematic preparation
