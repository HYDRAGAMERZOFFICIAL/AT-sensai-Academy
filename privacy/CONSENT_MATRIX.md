# Consent / Permission Matrix

| Activity | Person | Required? | Mechanism | Evidence | Withdrawal | System behavior |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Course enquiry | Visitor | Determine by legal basis/purpose | Notice + appropriate action | Request record | Stop unnecessary marketing | Process enquiry |
| Marketing email | User | Yes where required | Separate opt-in | Timestamp/version | Unsubscribe | Suppress marketing |
| WhatsApp marketing | User | Review | Separate opt-in | Timestamp/version | Opt-out | Suppress |
| Foundation admission | Minor + parent/guardian | Legal review | Approved parent process | Verification record | Defined workflow | Block progression if required |
| Student testimonial | Student/parent | Permission required | Consent form | Signed/electronic record | Defined | Unpublish where required |
| Student photograph | Student/parent | Permission required for publication | Consent form | Record | Defined | Remove/unpublish |
| Analytics | Visitor | Review technology/legal requirements | Consent mechanism if required | Consent log | Revoke | Disable optional tracker |

Do not combine service consent and marketing consent into one checkbox.
