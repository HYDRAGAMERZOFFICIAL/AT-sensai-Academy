## Notes

Regulations can change. Re-check official sources immediately before production launch and periodically after launch.

The DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 use phased commencement. Build the application to satisfy the full intended control set rather than waiting for a future commencement date.

CERT-In directions include incident reporting and information-security practices. Applicability and exact obligations should be confirmed for the institution and infrastructure.

The IT Rules should not automatically be treated as applying to an ordinary informational coaching website. Reassess if the product introduces public posts, comments, forums, user uploads, messaging between users, or other intermediary-like functionality.
