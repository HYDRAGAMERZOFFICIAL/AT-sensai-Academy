# Research notes — September 2026

This project kit was prepared after checking current official Indian sources.

## Important findings

### DPDP
MeitY lists the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 and an enforcement timeline. The Act and Rules have phased commencement, so the application should be engineered toward the complete control set rather than relying on a delayed compliance date.

### IT Rules
MeitY's current IT Rules page shows the 2021 rules and later amendments/updates. Ordinary institutional websites should not automatically assume they are intermediaries; applicability must be reassessed if the product adds public user-generated content or similar intermediary functionality.

### CERT-In
CERT-In maintains directions under Section 70B concerning information-security practices, incident prevention/response/reporting and related matters. Maintain an incident-response process and have counsel/security assess exact applicability.

### Consumer protection
The Department of Consumer Affairs maintains the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and related advertising/dark-pattern guidance. If online admissions/payments are enabled, have the business model reviewed for applicability and implement transparent pricing/refund/cancellation and non-deceptive UX.

### Copyright
India Code lists the Copyright Act, 1957. The project therefore includes an asset/license register and prohibits copying third-party coaching content without permission.
