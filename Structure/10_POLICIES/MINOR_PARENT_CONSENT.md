# Minor / Parent-Guardian Consent Specification

Applicable to the school Foundation programme.

Before production, define:
- Which services are available to minors
- What personal data is collected
- What requires parent/guardian consent
- How consent is obtained and recorded
- How consent can be withdrawn
- Who may contact the minor
- Rules for photographs/videos/testimonials
- Rules for public student profiles/results
- Data retention/deletion
- Escalation/contact process

Do not design a child-facing data collection flow based on assumptions. Obtain institution/legal approval first.
