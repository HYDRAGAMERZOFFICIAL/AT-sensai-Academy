# Legal / Policy Set

Prepare institution-approved versions of:
1. Privacy Policy
2. Terms & Conditions
3. Refund & Cancellation Policy
4. Admission Terms
5. Website Disclaimer
6. Cookie / Tracking Notice where applicable
7. Student/Parent Consent Notice
8. Photo/Video/Testimonial Consent
9. Intellectual Property / Content Use Rules
10. Scholarship/Discount Terms if applicable
11. Online class/recording terms if applicable

These are templates/checklists, not legal advice. Final wording must be reviewed for the actual institution, processing activities, vendors and business model.
