# Refund & Cancellation Policy — DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

Institution must decide and approve:
- Cooling-off/cancellation rules if applicable
- Registration fee treatment
- Tuition refund conditions
- Partial refund calculation
- Non-refundable items
- Batch transfer
- Course transfer
- Missed classes
- Academy cancellation
- Payment gateway reversal timelines
- Refund request method
- Evidence/document requirements
- Processing timelines

Never promise a refund amount/timeline until the institution confirms it.
