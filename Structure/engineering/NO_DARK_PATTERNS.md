# Anti-Dark-Pattern Rules

Do not implement:

- fake countdown timers;
- fake "only 2 seats left" messages;
- hidden mandatory charges;
- pre-selected marketing consent;
- confusing opt-out flows;
- disguised advertisements;
- forced account creation for simple enquiries unless necessary;
- misleading button labels;
- fake testimonials;
- fake reviews;
- misleading discount percentages;
- visual tricks that make refusal difficult;
- cancellation flows designed to obstruct users.

If the institution wants to display "limited seats", it must be based on an actual operational limit and remain accurate.
