# Cookie & Tracking Notice — DRAFT

**STATUS: LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED**

## Principles

Only deploy analytics/marketing tracking that has been approved.

Classify technologies as:
- strictly necessary;
- functional;
- analytics;
- advertising/marketing.

## Engineering rules

- Do not load non-essential marketing trackers before the required consent state.
- Do not treat a user clicking "continue" as blanket consent.
- Keep consent records where required.
- Provide a way to revisit consent.
- Document every third-party tracker.
