# Minor / Parent-Guardian Consent Specification

**STATUS: LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED**

AT Sensei's Foundation course includes classes 8–10, so the product must be designed to handle minors.

## Design principles

- Minimize child data.
- Prefer parent/guardian contact for admission.
- Do not use a child's contact details as the sole basis for parent consent.
- Do not publicly expose minor profiles.
- Avoid targeted advertising/profiling of minors.
- Store consent evidence.
- Record consent version and timestamp.
- Provide a withdrawal/revocation workflow where required.
- Restrict access by role.

## Suggested registration flow

1. Student/parent selects Foundation.
2. System identifies that the learner may be under 18.
3. Parent/guardian information is collected where required.
4. Appropriate privacy notice is shown.
5. Parent/guardian consent is obtained through an approved verification mechanism.
6. Consent evidence is stored.
7. Admission can proceed only when required consent conditions are satisfied.

## Do not implement yet

Do not choose an identity-verification method for parent consent without legal/product approval.

Do not collect government IDs merely as a convenient workaround.

## Public content

Before publishing:
- student photo;
- name;
- result;
- testimonial;
- video;
- classroom identification;

obtain the required institutional and parent/guardian permission and record the permission scope.

## Reference

See the official DPDP Rules 2025 and the final legal review for the applicable consent requirements and exemptions.
