# Security Incident Response Plan

## Severity

### SEV-1
Likely unauthorized access to sensitive student/minor/payment/admin data.

### SEV-2
Significant service compromise or data exposure with limited scope.

### SEV-3
Security issue with low exposure or contained impact.

### SEV-4
Minor vulnerability or non-production issue.

## Response

1. Detect.
2. Record incident ID and time.
3. Contain.
4. Preserve evidence.
5. Assess affected systems/data.
6. Rotate credentials/secrets if necessary.
7. Patch/eradicate.
8. Recover.
9. Validate.
10. Notify required stakeholders/authorities according to applicable law and contractual obligations.
11. Document lessons learned.

## CERT-In

Maintain an operational process for assessing whether an incident is reportable and for meeting applicable CERT-In directions and timelines.

Do not promise a reporting timeline in public policy text unless legal/security review confirms the current requirement.

## Evidence

Maintain:
- incident timeline;
- affected accounts;
- affected data classes;
- logs;
- containment actions;
- remediation;
- notifications;
- final report.
