# AT Sensei Academy — Product Requirements

## Product goal

Create a modern, trustworthy coaching website that converts visitors into:
- course enquiries;
- workshop registrations;
- admission applications;
- approved online payments;
- enrolled students.

A future student portal may include courses, schedules, attendance, assignments, tests, results, materials, certificates and communication.

## Primary audiences

1. Banking aspirants.
2. SSC aspirants.
3. School Foundation students and their parents/guardians.
4. Students/aspirants seeking career and competitive-exam awareness.

- Home
- About
- Programs

- Foundation

- Mentors

- Disclaimer

## Core conversion actions

- Explore Program
- Register Now
- Enquire
- Book/Request Workshop
- Call
- WhatsApp
- Get Directions

## Admission flow

Visitor
→ course selection
→ enquiry/registration
→ eligibility/confirmation
→ payment (if enabled)
→ admission confirmation
→ batch allocation
→ student account/onboarding

## UX principles

- Clear information before asking for data.
- No surprise fees.
- No deceptive urgency.
- No forced marketing consent.
- Clear distinction between enquiry, registration and paid admission.
- Clear parent involvement for minors.
- Mobile-first.
- Accessible.
- Fast.
- Trustworthy.

## Non-goals for initial release

- User-generated public forums.
- Public student directories.
- Public minor profiles.
- Unverified claims.
- Collecting unnecessary identity documents.
- Storing card/payment credentials.
