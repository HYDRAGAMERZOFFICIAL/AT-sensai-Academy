# Content approval workflow

1. Developer/AI drafts content from approved source.
2. Institution owner reviews factual accuracy.
3. Legal reviewer reviews claims/policies where relevant.
4. Final approver signs off.
5. Content receives a version/date.
6. Production deployment references the approved version.

## Content states

- DRAFT
- INSTITUTION REVIEW
- LEGAL REVIEW
- APPROVED
- PUBLISHED
- RETIRED

## Never publish

- unsupported success statistics;
- fake scarcity;
- fake testimonials;
- unverified rankings;
- unverified affiliations;
- claims that an exam/job is guaranteed;
- copied copyrighted material;
- unlicensed images;
- unapproved legal text.
