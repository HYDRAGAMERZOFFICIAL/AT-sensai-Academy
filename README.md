## Source of truth
The supplied `AT Sensei Academy.pdf` is the primary source for existing academy content. Do not invent institutional facts. Any information not present in the catalog must be supplied and approved by AT Sensei Academy before publication.

## Important
This kit is a project/governance aid, not legal advice. Final policies, consent language, contracts, payment terms, privacy practices and regulatory obligations must be reviewed and approved by the institution and an appropriate legal/privacy professional before production.
