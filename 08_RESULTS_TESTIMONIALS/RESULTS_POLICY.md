# Results & Testimonials Governance

Do not publish performance claims until supported by institution records.

For each result claim store:
- Student name/approved display name
- Exam
- Year
- Result/rank/achievement
- Programme/batch
- Evidence reference
- Consent to publish
- Approval status

For testimonials:
- Exact approved testimonial
- Student/parent identity as approved
- Programme
- Photo/video consent
- Date obtained
- Revocation/contact method where applicable

Never generate fake testimonials or synthetic student success stories.
